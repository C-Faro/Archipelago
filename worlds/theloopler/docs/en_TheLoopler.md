# The Loopler

## Where is the options page?

You can find one either on the [GitHub latest release](https://github.com/C-Faro/Archipelago/releases/tag/v0.0.1-alpha1) or by using the Options Creator in the Archipelago Launcher.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Items which the player would normally acquire throughout the game have been moved around. Because of the item shuffle the player may need to use certain tracks, cars, charms, or any other item before they
would in the vanilla game. All level unlocks are also locations that could have an item in them.

## What is the goal of The Loopler when randomized?

You can choose from the following goals:
1. Reach Infinity
2. Reach Max Level
3. Complete All Tracks
4. Complete All Cars
5. Complete All Combinations of Tracks, Cars, and Loops
6. All of the Above

## What items and locations get shuffled?

All tracks, cars, loops, charms, cards, and gates can be shuffled, and all locations in the game which could
contain any of those items may have their contents changed.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Any of the items which can be shuffled may also be placed into another player's world. It is possible to choose to limit
certain items to your own world.

## What does another world's item look like in The Loopler?

Items belonging to other worlds are represented by the Archipelago logo itself in the shape of a wheel.
